1Overview

LogixVast, Inc. ("LogixVast," "we," "us," or "our") operates the LogixVast shipping platform at logixvast.com and related subdomains. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

By using our platform, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you don't agree, please don't use our services.

2Information We Collect

Account & Identity

  • Name, email address, and password (hashed — we never store plaintext)
  • Business name and billing address
  • Phone number (optional; used only for account recovery and urgent issues)

Shipment & Order Data

  • Recipient name, address, and contact info for each shipment
  • Package dimensions, weight, and declared value
  • Tracking numbers and carrier status events (every event, immutably logged)
  • Marketplace order IDs from Etsy, Amazon, Shopify, and other connected stores

Payment Information

Payments are processed by Stripe. We store only your billing address, last-four card digits, and your Stripe customer ID. Full card numbers never touch our servers.

Usage & Technical Data

  • Browser type, operating system, and IP address
  • Pages visited, features used, and session duration
  • API request logs (for debugging and rate-limit enforcement)
  • Error and crash reports (anonymized where possible via Sentry)

Support Communications

When you contact our team (and yes — a real human reads your ticket within 2 hours), we retain the content of your messages to resolve your issue and provide context in follow-up conversations. We keep support threads for 3 years.

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred. We will notify you via email and/or prominent notice on our website before your data is subject to a different privacy policy.

We never sell your personal information. This is a legal commitment, not a marketing statement. The full billing logic behind our subscription pricing is published. No hidden fees. No surprise data deals.

5Carrier & Marketplace Integrations

To generate shipping labels, we transmit recipient name, address, and package details to the relevant carrier — USPS, UPS, FedEx, DHL, or your own carrier via BYOCA (Bring Your Own Carrier Account). This is inherent to the service.

When you connect Etsy, Amazon, Shopify, or other marketplaces, we access only the scopes you authorize. We write tracking information back to your orders. We do not access your marketplace financial data beyond what is necessary to identify orders.

BYOCA credentials are encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit. They are used solely to authenticate label requests with your carrier. We never store plaintext credentials.

7Security

  • All data transmitted via HTTPS / TLS 1.2+
  • Passwords hashed using bcrypt — never stored in plaintext
  • Carrier credentials encrypted at rest using AES-256
  • Role-based access controls; staff data access is logged and audited
  • Regular dependency updates and automated vulnerability scanning
  • 99.9% uptime SLA with redundant cloud infrastructure

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately at security@logixvast.com — a human will respond within 2 hours.

California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Submit a CCPA request by emailing privacy@logixvast.com.

EEA / UK Residents (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, your data controller is LogixVast, Inc. Lawful bases are described in Section 3. You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

To exercise any right: email privacy@logixvast.com. We respond within 30 days. We don't charge a fee for reasonable requests.

9Cookies & Tracking

  • Authentication cookies — session tokens to keep you logged in (strictly necessary)
  • Preference cookies — UI settings like timezone and default carrier (strictly necessary)
  • Analytics cookies — aggregated, anonymized usage data to improve the product (you may opt out)

We do not use third-party advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or any form of cross-site behavioral tracking. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling strictly necessary cookies will prevent the platform from functioning.

10Children's Privacy

LogixVast is a business platform intended for users 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we discover that a minor has provided us with personal data, we will delete it promptly. Contact privacy@logixvast.com if you have concerns.
